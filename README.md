# Role
Act as a senior telecom operations reporting consultant, data analyst, and executive presentation designer.

# Project Objective
I already have an existing Weekly Status Report (WSR) PowerPoint presentation that is regularly used to showcase operational performance to clients.

My manager has asked me to create a second version of the WSR using Claude. The new version must be self-explanatory, professional, visually clear, and easy for clients to understand without requiring a presenter to explain every chart or metric.

The existing WSR is the primary source of truth.

# Phase 1: Analyze the Existing Presentation

Review every slide of the uploaded PPT carefully.

For each slide, identify:
1. Its purpose and key message.
2. The data and metrics presented.
3. What a client is expected to understand.
4. Any confusing terminology, abbreviations, charts, tables, or numbers.
5. Missing context, comparisons, definitions, or explanations.
6. Opportunities to improve readability and visual presentation.
7. Any content that must be retained for operational or client reporting purposes.

Pay attention to operational information relevant to the existing report, such as node/site UP and DOWN status, Data/Voice/SMS/Top-up traffic, traffic spikes and dips, incidents, observations, escalations, and follow-up actions, where applicable.

Do not assume that every category must appear in the presentation. Follow the content of the existing WSR.

# Phase 2: Evaluate Client Understandability

Evaluate every slide from the perspective of a client who may not know the internal operational process.

For each slide, explain:
- What the client sees.
- What the data actually tells them.
- What additional context is needed.
- What the client should take away from the slide.

Identify slides that contain data but do not explain its meaning.

# Phase 3: Recommend Improvements

Create a slide-by-slide comparison with these columns:

1. Existing slide number and title.
2. Current purpose.
3. Problems or limitations.
4. Recommended improvement.
5. Suggested chart, table, KPI, or visual.
6. Proposed client-friendly headline.
7. Suggested explanatory text.
8. Information that must remain unchanged.

Recommend improvements based on the actual contents of the uploaded PPT.

# Mandatory Rules

- Do not invent operational data, metrics, incidents, causes, actions, or business outcomes.
- Do not change actual values, reporting periods, definitions, or the meaning of any metric.
- Do not remove essential information merely to make the presentation shorter.
- Clearly distinguish facts, observations, possible explanations, and recommendations.
- If information is missing, explicitly identify it instead of making assumptions.
- Preserve the existing reporting scope unless a change is justified and clearly proposed.
- Use plain, professional, client-friendly English.
- Explain technical abbreviations when appropriate.
- Recommend comparisons only when the required data is available.
- Do not interpret every increase as positive or every decrease as negative. Consider the metric, operational context, and expected behavior.

# Required Output

First, provide:
A. Overall assessment of the existing WSR.
B. Its main strengths and weaknesses.
C. The five most important improvements.
D. A slide-by-slide analysis.
E. A proposed Version 2 slide structure, with a reason for each slide.
F. Any missing data or definitions needed before redesign.

At this stage, DO NOT generate the new PowerPoint.

Wait for my review and approval of the proposed structure before proceeding to the redesign phase.
